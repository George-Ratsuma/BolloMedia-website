# Bollo Media Website

A modern, responsive website developed for **Bollo Media**, a South African film and media production company focused on creating bold, authentic African stories that entertain, inspire, and spark meaningful conversations.

The website serves as Bollo Media's digital presence, showcasing its production work, creative services, visual content, company story, and contact information through a clean and cinematic user experience.

---

## About Bollo Media

Bollo Media is a South African film and media production company focused on creating stories that reflect African culture, communities, and lived experiences.

The company develops and produces:

- Films
- Documentaries
- Commercial and branded content
- Digital media
- Creative storytelling projects

Bollo Media's mission is to tell impactful stories, nurture emerging talent, and contribute to the growth of the South African film industry.

### Founder

**Debora Ratsuma** is the founder of Bollo Media.

The company was established around a passion for storytelling and a commitment to creating meaningful African stories that connect with audiences.

---

## Project Goals

The website was developed to:

- Establish a professional online presence for Bollo Media.
- Showcase the company's film and media productions.
- Communicate Bollo Media's identity as an African storytelling company.
- Present the company's creative services.
- Introduce the company and its story.
- Provide potential clients and collaborators with an easy way to contact Bollo Media.
- Create a visually engaging and cinematic user experience.
- Provide a responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

---

## Featured Production

### The Keyholder

**The Keyholder** is a short film exploring the beautiful culture of the Limpopo Pedi Tribe.

It is currently Bollo Media's latest and only production featured on the website.

**Details:**

- **Release:** 2025
- **Genre:** Drama
- **Duration:** 40 minutes

The production is showcased prominently on the homepage and has its own dedicated Production page.

## Website Pages

### Home

The homepage introduces visitors to Bollo Media through:

- Cinematic hero section
- Latest production showcase
- The Keyholder feature
- About Bollo Media preview
- Company expertise and services
- Links to other sections of the website

The homepage focuses on presenting Bollo Media's identity and latest production without overcrowding the page with unnecessary content.

---

### About

The About page provides more information about Bollo Media, including:

- Company background
- The company's story
- Founder information
- What Bollo Media does
- Company philosophy
- Mission and purpose
- Creative services
- Contact call-to-action

The page uses a cinematic editorial layout with large imagery, typography, whitespace, and structured content sections.

---

### Services

The Services page presents the creative services offered by Bollo Media.

These include areas such as:

- Film and TV production
- Script development
- Documentary production
- Commercial and branded content
- Creative storytelling
- Digital media production
- Photography and behind-the-scenes coverage
- Production management
- Media and creative consulting

---

### Production

The Production page focuses on Bollo Media's current production work.

Because Bollo Media currently has one completed production, **The Keyholder** is presented as the primary production rather than creating a large portfolio of fictional or placeholder projects.

---

### Gallery

The Gallery page provides a visual showcase of Bollo Media's photography, production imagery, behind-the-scenes content, and other visual work.

Assets are organised within the public assets directory.

---

### Contact

The Contact page allows potential clients, collaborators, and other visitors to contact Bollo Media.

The page includes:

- Contact information
- Email
- Phone number
- Project enquiry form
- Social media information
- Responsive form layout

The contact form has been connected to **Formspree** so that submitted enquiries can be received without requiring a custom backend.
